FAQs

Questions, answered

Is Zoho Books GST-compliant for Indian businesses?+
Yes. The India edition of Zoho Books is built for GST — you can raise GST invoices, generate GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, and file returns directly. We configure your GSTIN, tax rates and HSN/SAC codes during setup.
Do I need a separate account for each app?+
You sign in with one Zoho account and add the apps you need. Books, Payroll and Inventory share the same customer, item and tax data, so they work together as one connected system. You can start with just one and add the others later.
Can you migrate my existing data from Tally or Excel?+
Yes. Migration is part of what we do — masters, opening balances, item lists, customers and historical transactions are moved and verified so you start clean on Zoho.
Do you provide setup and training in Odisha?+
Absolutely. As your local Zoho partner we handle implementation, hands-on staff training and ongoing support on the ground in Bhubaneswar and across Odisha — reachable in your own timezone.
